**Why these, in this order**
- **Timing comes first.** The 8 + 2 week lead time means orders for even Diwali week were due by 24 Aug. Row 1 decides how much of everything below is possible.
- **Curtains carry the units.** They are 67,307 of 79,968 baseline units (84%) and 11,009 of the 16,872 net extra units. That is why the HM152-063 family and the high-lift curtains rank above the higher-percentage lines.
- **Decorative lines lift the most in percentage terms.** Curtains rise only +16% net over 12 weeks, against +56% to +62% for Bedsheet, Cushion, Bolster and Table Linen. Their volumes are small, so they rank below the curtain rows.
- **The 1.79x average is the sizing basis.** The forecast gives no weekly multiplier by category, so rows 5 and 6 use the total multiplier. The 2025 SKU lifts (rows 2-4) are the better guide where a SKU has history.
- **The sale is front-loaded.** Week 1 is already 1.79x (11,902) and the peak is 2.0x, so there is no time to top up mid-sale. After Diwali, demand runs about 21% below normal for three weeks.
- **Only one 2025 SKU has stopped selling.** That is HMBL-078-16x32-S2, which row 7 replaces. The other 24 on the list are flagged as still selling.

**What would change this plan**
- **Your actual stock position.** If a batch is already due before 5 Oct, allocate it in row order. If total stock is short of 47,625 units, fill rows 2-4 first. I have no inventory data.
- **Amazon's confirmed sale dates.** 5 Oct is inferred from 2025 and Diwali on 8 Nov. A later opening moves every date here.
- **The first week of the sale (5-11 Oct).** The forecast is 11,902, roughly 10,236-13,568 allowing for the ~14% backtest error. Outside that band, the 2025 pattern is not repeating.
- **Stock-out flags on HM162-173-BGE-8FT, HM162-173-BGE-6FT, HMCC-16-3005-PNK-S2 and HMMP-72x78-GRY.** If sales were capped by stock, true demand is higher than shown.
- **Festive history for the growth SKUs.** Ten of the 12 are 348-364 days old, so they were probably on sale in the 2025 window. Only the two HM162-173 SKUs are certain to have no history. Pull each SKU's own 2025 lift before finalising rows 5-6.
- **Winter units.** The Nov-Dec 2025 units for Comforter/Quilt and Duvet Cover would let row 10 be sized.
- **The region forecasts.** They sum to 82,292 baseline units against 79,968 in the total, so use them for relative weighting only.
